PITCH & DESIGN DOCUMENT

"A man cannot outrun his shadow, no matter how fast he drives."

Lead Designer: SUNNY RAJPUT
Version: 1.0 (Professional Pitch)
Genre: Psychological Thriller / Action-Driving

GAME SYNOPSIS

Zero Drag is the story of Rupesh (21), who infiltrates the heart of a criminal drug syndicate to avenge the death of his family. The game explores the thin line between justice and mania, forcing the player to question their own perception of the "enemy."

PROPER MISSION LINEUP (M01 - M10)

MISSION 01: ASH NIGHT PROLOGUE
CUTSCENE: INTRO
Night. Rainfall. The crackle of huge flames. The camera slowly orbits Rupesh, who is kneeling on the wet pavement. The bright orange of the burning house reflects in his dry, unblinking eyes. Neighbors are seen in the distance, their voices muffled, like they are underwater.
NPC #1: "The drug cartel... they wanted this land."
NPC #2: "Poor kid. He's got no one left."
Screen Fades to Black. Text appears: 3 MONTHS LATER. Rupesh is shown in a small, dark garage, cleaning a bike. His friend stands in the doorway.
Friend: "Forget them, Rupesh. They are monsters."
Rupesh: "I'll reach them... whether they are monsters or gods."
MISSION 02: ENTRY LEVEL RUNNER THE FIRST STEP

Rupesh meets Shree, a low-level supplier in a shady parking lot. Player must complete 3 manual parcel deliveries to earn trust.

Mechanic: The Trust Gauge Deliveries must be clean. No damages, no police attention. Shree evaluates your speed.
Shree: "You drive like you're not afraid to die. That's dangerous... or very useful. We'll see."
MISSION 03: RISK DELIVERY THE BRIDGE CROSSING

A midnight delivery across the main city bridge. Police patrols are heavy due to a city-wide curfew.

Mechanic: Stealth Driving Player must kills the engine and coast in silence to avoid patrols. The bridge is the only way in.
MISSION 04: HEAT INCREASE THE ESCAPE

A routine drop turns into a setup. Police sirens surround the block. Rupesh must find a risky shortcut through a narrow alleyway to escape the perimeter.

Shree (Radio): "Get out of there, Rupesh! I didn't know the Heat was this high!"
MISSION 05: PROMOTION EYE IN THE SKY

Multiple drops in high-end areas. First appearance of the Shadow Boss.

CUTSCENE: BALCONY
A man stands on a penthouse balcony, camera stays behind him. You only see his expensive suit and a glass of scotch. He watches Rupesh's bike fly through the traffic below.
Villain: "He has zero drag. No friction with his conscience. I like him."
MISSION 06: LOYALTY TEST SUICIDE RUN

Shree offers a mission that everyone else refused—a delivery straight into the rival gang's territory. Rupesh accepts without even asking for the payment amount.

MISSION 07: PERSONAL RISK THE GHOST ROUTE

The route passes through "Ash Street"—where Rupesh's family died. This is a deep narrative mission.

Mental Health System: Glitch Overlay The screen flickers and colors distort. Audio becomes distorted as the house remains in sight.
Rupesh (Whisper): "I can still smell the smoke. I'm coming home... soon."
MISSION 08: IMPOSSIBLE RUN THE GATEKEEPER

High-speed highway convoy protection. Player must fend off attackers while maintaining 180kmph.

Shree: "Boss wants to meet you. You're the only one who survived the filter."
MISSION 09: PREPARATION THE BLACKOUT

No driving. Rupesh walks through an underground gun market. He buys a custom black handgun.

CUTSCENE: ROOFTOP
Rupesh stands on a windy rooftop, cleaning the gun. He stares at a photo of his family. He lights it on fire. As it burns, his face changes... for a split second, he looks terrifyingly calm.
Rupesh: "Tomorrow... the cycle ends. No more drag."
THE GRAND FINALE (TWIST)

Rupesh enters the penthouse. The Boss is waiting. Slow walk sequence.

"You blame me for that night? You think I took them away?"
Boss turns around—He is Rupesh.

THE REVELATION: Rupesh has Multiple Personality Disorder. The Boss isn't a drug dealer—he is the manifestation of Rupesh's suppressed ego. Rupesh burned the house himself in a dissociative blackout because his family "limited" his potential. The whole game was his Hero personality trying to cope with the monster he had become.

The final shot shows the police files on the table. The "syndicate" was just Rupesh alone, committing crimes across the city in his other state.
