The Story Narrative

Nobody Wakes Up Deciding To Become A Villain. It Happens One Choice At A Time.

Meet Rupesh — jobless, debt-ridden, desperate. When Salim Bhai hires him for a closed package delivery inside a dark alley warehouse, he doesn't ask questions. But when he meets the volatile cartel distributor Vikram and learns about "Zero" — a highly illegal focus amplifier circulating the city — there's no turning back. From that night on, every shootout, every negotiation, and every dead syndicate enforcer pushes Rupesh closer to the ultimate friction-free state.

01
Act 1 — Missions 1–3
"Just This Once"
A simple package exchange inside an abandoned rail yard. Vikram reveals the true horror of the Zero network. When a local street contact spots the trade, Rupesh faces his first real crucible.
⚡ Story Cruxes
🟡 Squeal to Sharma — maintain morals, forfeit gang trust, receive protection.
🔴 Intimidate the witness — enter the gang network, gain reputation.
🔴 Eliminate the target — drop your emotional drag. Real violence begins.

Act 2 — Missions 4–7
"The Network expansion"
Vikram takes Rupesh deep inside safehouses and distribution hubs. Meanwhile, **Inspector Sharma** launches a heavy dragnet to capture Rupesh. Loyalty is tested from both ends.
⚡ Story Cruxes
🟡 Double-cross Vikram — wire-taps for Sharma, extremely high hazard.
🔴 Wipe out Sharma's leads — solidify position as an elite syndicate enforcer.
03
Act 3 — Missions 8–12
"Zero or Hero"
The hidden cartel boss issues an ultimatum. Standing at the high-rise peak of a concrete jungle, Rupesh makes his final play against the syndicates and the law.

Frictionless Newtonian Locomotion & Physics Jumps
Built completely on custom mathematics using a manual Unity CharacterController. Experience instant WASD movement, split-body mouse looks with vertical pitch locks, and precise kinematic leaps mapped to $u = \sqrt{h \times -2 \times g}$ equations. Zero delay, zero drag, infinite kinetic speed.
Frame-rate independent movement physics using smoothed Time.deltaTime
Exorcist clamp: strictly locks camera vertical yaw at $+90^{\circ}/-90^{\circ}$
Precision sphere-cast ground tests utilizing custom LayerMasks
✅ Live in Alpha
💥
Cinematic Fracture Destruction
Deliver instant physical impact. On lethal gunshots, solid enforcer models instantly swap to pre-fractured mesh structures. An explosion force radial blow-out spreads shards across 3D space.
✅ Live in Alpha
🎯
Hitscan Shooting & Surface Particles
Project mathematically exact hitscan rays. Contact points generate concrete/blood sparks oriented dynamically straight out of surfaces using LookRotation normal vectors.

ScoreManager Singleton Economy
Decoupled manager counting street enemies dynamically using tag filters on start. Handles real-time HUD TextMeshPro updates, laundering payouts, and XP states.

Dialogue System Live — Vikram's Zero Reveal Complete
Dialogue Mission 1 Story NPC Path AI
▼

A huge step forward. Zero Drag transitioned from a basic physics sandbox into a **fully interactive story experience** today. The custom typewriter dialogue system is now operational, displaying speaker names and firing callbacks on dialogue completion to unlock warehouse doors.

Salim Bhai coordinates the opening dialogue with Rupesh, offering cash for a closed container. **Vikram's** meeting inside the rail yards is an 18-line cinematic asset, introducing the "Zero" focus substance with the cold prompt: "Makes things quiet. Inside your head."

Additionally, I built NPC blocking AI: civilian/gang agents now stop dynamically if the player obstructs their path, using dot product direction tests to trigger agent halts before resuming their patrol loops.

FEB
15
2026
Physics & Impact Core Complete — Swapped Solid for Shards
Physics Fracture ScoreManager Hitscan
▼

Focused on combat loops. Deployed the **Fake Switch deconstruction technique** to replace classic ragdolls with highly rewarding physical shatters. When a thug's health goes to zero, the main GameObject is immediately disabled, instantiating a broken mesh prefab consisting of 12 separate colliders. An explosion force is broadcast, pushing the fragments outward dynamically.

The global **ScoreManager Singleton** was wired to dynamically track combat states. It gathers all hostile tags on scene load, removing the need for manual inspector references and preventing UI desyncs.

  • AddExplosionForce radial offsets

  • ScoreManager static singletons
  • Impact normal spark rotations
  • TextMeshPro HUD updates
